Good Care Health Centre

Disease Prevention

General prevention information covering everyday habits, screening awareness and ways to reduce common health risks.

🦟

Malaria

Reduce mosquito exposure and seek testing promptly when malaria is suspected. Prevention advice should follow local health guidance.

🩸

Diabetes

Healthy eating patterns, regular physical activity and appropriate screening can help people manage risk. Ask a qualified professional about your individual situation.

🧼

Infection Prevention

Hand hygiene, safe food and water practices, appropriate vaccination and staying home when unwell can reduce the spread of many infections.

❤️

HIV & Sexual Health

Use prevention methods, seek testing when appropriate and talk to qualified health professionals about confidential sexual-health care.

🫁

Tuberculosis

Persistent cough and other concerning symptoms should be assessed by a health professional. Follow local testing and treatment guidance.

👩

Women’s Health

Routine health checks, reproductive-health services and timely attention to unusual symptoms can support women’s health.

For current disease guidance, use trusted health authorities such as the World Health Organization or Zambia Ministry of Health, and speak with a qualified professional for personal advice.
