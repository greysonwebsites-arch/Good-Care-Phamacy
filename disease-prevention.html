Diseases & Prevention

Understand common health conditions, learn practical prevention steps and know when it is important to seek professional care.

Featured Health Topics

Good Care Pharmacy can use this section for community education on conditions commonly discussed in primary health care.

MALARIA
🦟

Malaria

Prevention includes reducing mosquito exposure and using recommended mosquito-control measures. Fever or other concerning symptoms should be assessed promptly where malaria occurs.

See: WHO Malaria

DIABETES
🩸

Diabetes

Healthy eating patterns, regular physical activity, avoiding tobacco and appropriate health checks can support prevention and management.

See: WHO Diabetes

INFECTIONS
🧼

Infection Prevention

Hand hygiene, safe food and water, appropriate vaccination and infection-control practices are important tools for reducing avoidable infections.

See: WHO Infection Prevention

HIV & STIs
❤️

HIV & Sexual Health

Testing, prevention, safer-sex practices and timely access to appropriate care are important. Reliable information and confidential professional services can help.

See: WHO HIV/AIDS

TB
🫁

Tuberculosis

Persistent cough and other concerning symptoms should be assessed by a health professional. TB is preventable and treatable.

See: WHO Tuberculosis

WOMEN'S HEALTH
🌸

Women's Health

Preventive care can include appropriate screening, vaccination, reproductive health services and timely attention to unusual symptoms.

See: WHO Women's Health
